# Product — Pravah

## Overview

Pravah is an India-first zero-decision fitness and nutrition app.

The product promise is simple: reduce daily health friction by making the obvious next decision for the user. Meals, workout framing, recovery posture, and grocery guidance should feel curated rather than manually assembled.

## Target user

- urban Indian adults who want structure without calorie-counting overhead
- users who value guided defaults more than deep customization
- people trying to stay consistent through normal workweek chaos

## Core product ideas

- fewer decisions, not more dashboards
- Indian food context by default
- fitness and nutrition guidance should feel connected
- recovery context matters, not just ideal-plan adherence

## Current product surfaces

- Today
- Fuel / meals
- Flow / workout
- Rest / recovery
- Data / insights
- Grocery
- Guided onboarding
- Auth and account management

## Current onboarding capture

The production onboarding flow currently persists backend-backed meal preference defaults only:

- diet type
- goal
- meal count
- prep time window

Anything richer should be added later as progressive profiling, not by bloating first-run setup.

## Non-goals

- manual calorie logging
- fully custom workout authoring
- social/community product mechanics
- dark mode in v1
